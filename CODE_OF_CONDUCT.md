# Community Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Pledge

We, as members, maintainers, faculty, alumni, and contributors of the **CSCPDUAM Community** (Department of Computer Science, Pandit Deendayal Upadhyaya Adarsha Mahavidyalaya), pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy department and open-source environment.

---

## 2. Standards of Behavior

### Positive Behaviors

Examples of behavior that contribute to creating a positive environment include:

- **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward other students, faculty, and contributors.
- **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- **Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback**.
- **Accepting responsibility** and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- **Focusing on what is best** for the community and departmental learning environment.
- **Encouraging peer learning** and mentoring junior members in computer science concepts and open-source practices.

### Unacceptable Behaviors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment in any form.
- Publishing others' private information (such as personal email, phone numbers, or credentials) without explicit permission ("doxxing").
- Plagiarism, academic dishonesty, or submitting malicious code/vulnerabilities.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

---

## 3. Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers and Department of Computer Science faculty are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers and faculty have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, discussions, commits, code edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for enforcement decisions when appropriate.

---

## 4. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces associated with CSCPDUAM Community, including:

- The custom web application and GitHub Discussions feed.
- The official GitHub repository (`cscpduam-org/community`).
- Departmental workshops, events, and online discussion forums.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

---

## 5. Reporting Guidelines & Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community maintainers or department head.

- **Primary Contact:** Community Maintainers & Faculty Committee (`cscpduam.community@gmail.com` or through department administrators).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All maintainers and faculty are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### Enforcement Process

1. **Correction:** A private written warning providing clarity on the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning with explicit consequences for continued violation.
3. **Temporary Suspension:** A temporary ban from interacting with community discussions or submitting code for a specified period.
4. **Permanent Ban:** A permanent ban from the community platform and departmental open-source organization for repeated or severe violations.
